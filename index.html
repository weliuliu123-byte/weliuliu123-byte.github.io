My Contribution vs. Team Scope

In this 5-member baseband communication project (teammates built Hamming codec, BPSK mod/demod, LFSR noise injector, and Vivado synthesis analysis), I owned system-level integration, the bit-flip error channel, and system verification.

  • System Top-Level (tx_rx_dsp_top.v): Integrated all 6 DSP modules under synchronous active-low reset (i_rst_n), latching i_noise_level/i_err_mode on w_start_gated (i_start && !r_busy) and emitting a 1-clock o_done pulse.

  • System Verification & Chapter 1: Verified tb_bit_flip_channel (1,537 checks), tb_tx_rx_dsp_top (154,373 checks / 384 tx / 24,576 samples), and UART E2E simulation checks with 0 errors, and authored Chapter 1.
